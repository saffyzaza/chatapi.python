# ULTRA DETAILED: Research - Factors of Raw Fish Consumption (Provincial Study, Sisaket)

## Research Overview
- **Title:** Factors Related to the Consumption of Raw Fish-Uncooked/Half-cooked among the People in Sisaket Province 2016.
- **Published:** 2019 (Journal of Health Science).
- **Study Area:** All 22 districts of Sisaket Province.
- **Sample Size:** 900 people (Aged 15+), 30 per district.

## Provincial Situation (2016)
- **Consumption Rate:** 69.4% of participants reported consuming raw or uncooked freshwater fish.
- **Frequency:** 38.7% consume raw fish less than once a month.
- **Main Source of Infection:** **Papaya salad (Som Tam) with pickled raw fish sauce (Pla-ra)** (94.2%).

## Misconceptions and Beliefs
- **Frequency Myth:** 56.1% believe that eating raw fish "only a little" or "infrequently" will not lead to liver fluke infection.
- **Traditional Culture:** 39.4% believe raw fish consumption is a cultural heritage that should be preserved.
- **Taste Preference:** 29.9% prefer the taste of raw fish over cooked versions.
- **Fatalism:** 20.3% express a fatalistic view ("Eat or don't eat, you die anyway").

## Statistical Risk Factors (Odds Ratio - ORc)
- **Gender:** Males are **1.4 times** more likely to consume raw fish than females.
- **Alcohol:** Drinkers are **1.8 times** more likely (raw fish often served as a snack with alcohol).
- **Smoking:** Smokers are **2.1 times** more likely.
- **Knowledge Gap:** Those unaware that raw fish causes Cholangiocarcinoma (CCA) are **32.0 times** more likely to consume it.
- **Occupation:** Agricultural workers are **3.9 times** more likely than government officers.
- **Socio-economics:** Lower education, lower individual income, and lower family income are all strongly correlated with higher raw fish consumption.

## Health Statistics (Sisaket 2016)
- **CCA Deaths:** 406 deaths (27.58 per 100,000 population).
- **Liver Fluke Prevalence:** 11.9% statewide. Highest in Wang Hin (45.2%), Sri Rattana (32.8%), and Nam Kliang (28.3%).
- **Intermediate Hosts:** 3 types of local freshwater fish were found positive for liver fluke larvae (Metacercaria): Pla Mae Sa-daeng, Pla Sroi Nok Khao, and Pla Tapian Sai.

## Strategic Recommendations
- **Educational Campaigns:** Urgently bridge the knowledge gap between raw fish and CCA.
- **Targeted Interventions:** Focus on middle-aged males in the agricultural sector.
- **Culture Transformation:** Promote "Isan Tae Kin Tae Pla-ra Suk" (True Isan people only eat cooked Pla-ra).
- **Collaborative Governance:** Integrate the "Sisaket Model" for liver fluke control into district-level quality of life committees (DQC/พชอ.).
