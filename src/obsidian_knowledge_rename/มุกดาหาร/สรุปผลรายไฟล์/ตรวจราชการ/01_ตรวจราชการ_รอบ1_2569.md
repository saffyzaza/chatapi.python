## 1. Vision and Policy for 2569
The overarching theme for FY 2569 is **"10 เต็ม 10 @ มุกดาหาร"** (10 out of 10 @ Mukdahan), supported by **10 Core Operational Principles**:
1.  Correct and rapid policy adoption.
2.  Host precedes the guest (Proactive hosting).
3.  Prioritize district-level leadership meetings.
4.  Collaborative work with District Officers (Nai Amphoe).
5.  Fast Track Service systems.
6.  4T Framework: Trust, Teamwork, Technology, Talent.
7.  Action oriented: "Do fast, do well, show results."

## 4. Key Strategies & Innovations (PIRAB Model)

### Maternal and Child Health
*   **Set Zero MMR:** Implementing "4P for Plan" (Pregnancy, ANC, Delivery, Postpartum).
*   **One Province One Labor Room:** Using Zoom for real-time consultation between community hospitals and specialist teams at Mukdahan General Hospital.
*   **Mom Muk App:** Digital tracking for pregnant women and child growth.

### Child IQ and Development
*   **Area-Based Target:** Average IQ > 106 by 2574.
*   **Current IQ (Grade 1):** 108.61 (Tested in 2568).
*   **Intervention:** Progesterone for high-risk preterm labor; cervical length measurement for all pregnant women.

### Elderly Care
*   **LTC (Long Term Care):** 100% coverage of "Age-Friendly Communities" in 38 sub-districts.
*   **Wellness Plan:** 58.85% of elderly have a registered wellness plan.

## 5. Administrative Instructions & Solutions
1.  **Data Quality Audit:** Inconsistent coding for vaccines (MMR2) and dental checks must be resolved by the Digital Health team.
2.  **Referral System:** Strengthen the "Fast Track OB Refer" for critical maternity cases.
3.  **Community Engagement:** Use "อสม. บัดดี้" (Volunteer Buddies) to follow up on late child development cases (DSPM).
4.  **Resource Balancing:** Caregiver ratio in Mueang district is currently 1:10; goal is to reduce to 1:5 through more training sessions.
