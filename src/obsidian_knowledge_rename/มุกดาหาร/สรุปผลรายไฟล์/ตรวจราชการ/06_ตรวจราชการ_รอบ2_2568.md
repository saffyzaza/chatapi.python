## 5. Strategic Directives & Solutions
1.  **NCD Screening Crisis:** The failure to meet DM/HT screening targets is attributed to poor data linkage between primary care and the central HDC. **Instruction:** Deploy "Mobile Screening Units" and implement real-time dashboards for district officers.
2.  **Environmental Health:** While all hospitals passed GREEN & CLEAN standards, only 1 passed the "Challenge" level. **Instruction:** Focus on Medical Waste Management and Solar Energy investment for FY 2569.
3.  **Maternal Health:** Expand "Mom Muk Model" to include 1:1 volunteer buddies for every pregnant woman to prevent late ANC entry.
