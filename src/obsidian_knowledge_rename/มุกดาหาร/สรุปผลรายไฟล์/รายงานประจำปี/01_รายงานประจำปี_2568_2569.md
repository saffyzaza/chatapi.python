## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Framework
Mukdahan province aligns its health development with the **20-Year National Strategy (2018-2037)**, focusing on human resource potential and the "Thailand 4.0" vision.

### Key Strategic Pillars:
*   **PP&P Excellence:** Health promotion, disease prevention, and consumer protection.

*   **Mukdahan Model:** A provincial integration strategy involving the Provincial Administrative Organization (PAO) and local communities to drive health KPIs.

## 7. Problem Analysis (SWOT)
*   **Weakness:** Caregiver training targets not met (79.33%); High workload in urban centers; Incomplete digital data entry in remote areas.
*   **Threat:** Border location leads to drug trafficking risks and cross-border infectious disease transmission.
