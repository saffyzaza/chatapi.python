# ULTRA DETAILED: Mental Health and Drug Status - Sisaket (2025-2029)

## Overview
This report provides a comprehensive analysis of the mental health and drug-related health services in Sisaket Province, focusing on suicide prevention, crisis management (MCATT), and child mental health development.

## 1. Mental Health and Suicide Situation
- **Trend:** Suicide rates in Sisaket have shown an upward trend over the past 3 years.
- **Statistics:**
    - 2022: 105 cases (7.22 per 100,000)
    - 2023: 107 cases (7.37 per 100,000)
    - 2024: 117 cases (8.02 per 100,000)
- **Demographics:** Primarily males, working-age, and elderly. Common occupation: Agriculture/Farming.
- **Methods:** Hanging is the most common method.
- **Risk Factors:** 
    - Psychiatric disorders (42.86%, mostly Schizophrenia).
    - Alcohol use (35.71%).
    - Chronic physical illnesses (25%, e.g., cancer, kidney failure, liver cirrhosis).
- **Triggers:** Family conflicts (50%), suffering from physical/mental illness (46.43%).
- **High-Risk Districts:** Mueang Sisaket, Kanthararom, and Khukhan.
- **Youth (Under 20):** 60.71% of youth attempts are linked to psychiatric issues (mostly depression). Triggers include conflicts with close ones (59%).

## 2. Sisaket Model (SSK Model) - "Save Life Together"
Sisaket has implemented the **SSK Model** to address suicide and mental health:
- **S: Save Life Together** - Monitoring high-risk groups (suicide attempters, violent psychiatric patients).
- **S: Sustainable Systems** - Provincial-level mechanism meeting monthly; policy cascaded to district and sub-district levels.
- **K: Knowledge Access** - Analyzing suicide cases (Psychological Autopsy) to develop local communication and mental health literacy.
- **Motto:** "Sisaket People: Good Health, Heart Vaccine, No Suicide."

## 3. MCATT (Mental Health Crisis Assessment and Treatment Team)
- **Border Conflict Management:** Sisaket faced border instability (Thai-Cambodian border). MCATT provided psychological first aid (PFA) and long-term monitoring for residents in Phu Sing, Kantharalak, and Khun Han.
- **Impact of Conflict:** High levels of chronic stress, anxiety, and PTSD, especially in children and the elderly.
- **Response:** Integrated care between health, security, and religious sectors. Established "Community Mind Recovery Centers" (e.g., in Phrai Phatthana).

## 4. Drug Control and Integration
- **Integration:** Mental health work is integrated with **TO BE NUMBER ONE** and Drug Control programs across all districts.
- **SMI-V System:** Monitoring psychiatric patients with high risk of violence (SMI-V) and those with co-occurring drug use issues.
- **Monitoring:** Post-hospitalization monitoring and community-based treatment.

## 5. Child and Adolescent Mental Health (KPIs 2025)
Sisaket monitors the access to services for neurodevelopmental disorders:
- **Autism (ASD):** 71.84% access (Target 60%) - **Passed**.
- **ADHD:** 61.72% access (Target 42%) - **Passed**.
- **Intellectual Disability (ID):** 47.00% access (Target 22%) - **Passed**.
- **Developmental Delay (0-5 years):** 15.08% access (Target 20%) - **Failed**.

## 6. Success Factors and Challenges
### Success Factors:
- Strong leadership and policy commitment from the Provincial Governor.
- Collaborative governance across various sectors (Public Health, Security, Local Government, Religious).
- Community participation (VHVs/Health Watchers).
- Use of technology (Local Mental Health Dashboard, "JaiUn Border" app).

### Challenges:
- Shortage of specialized personnel (Child/Adolescent Psychiatrists).
- Data integration issues between different systems (HDC vs. 506S).
- High workload for community health workers.
- Stigma associated with mental health issues.

## 7. Future Directions (2026-2029)
- Developing **"Community-Based Proactive Mental Health Systems"**.
- Establishing "Sub-district Mental Health Watch Units".
- Enhancing "Digital Mental Health Platforms" for tele-mental health.
- Scaling up the **"Sisaket Model: Heart Vaccine for Border Areas"**.
- Integrating mental health into the **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** (SDG 3, 10, 16, 17).
