# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 2/2024 - Sisaket

## Overview
Comprehensive results from the second round of provincial inspection for Sisaket (June 2024). This report tracks the full implementation of the "30 Baht Everywhere" policy and the "Smart Hospital" initiative.

## 1. Strategic Policies (Agenda Based)
- **30 Baht Everywhere:** Full activation. Patients in Sisaket can access any healthcare unit in the network using their ID card.
- **Prison Health (Rajathan Punsook):** 80.2% of inmates screened for oral health. 100% of new inmates screened for TB, HIV, and Syphilis.
- **Monk Health:** 50% of monks in the province received health check-ups.

## 2. Disease Control and NCDs
- **NCD Remission:** Focus on Diabetes and Hypertension reversal. 63.27% of HT patients have well-controlled blood pressure.
- **Tuberculosis:** Coverage rate: 60.9%. Treatment success rate for new smear-positive cases: 34.04% (Ongoing).
- **Cancer Care:**
    - Breast Cancer: 51.67% of the target group (30-70 years) screened.
    - Cervical Cancer: 14.11% of the target group (30-60 years) screened via HPV DNA.
    - Liver/Bile Duct: 131.86% of the quarterly target for Ultrasound screening achieved.

## 3. Digital Health and Innovation
- **Smart Hospital:** 22/22 hospitals linked to the PHR (Personal Health Record) system.
- **Tele-medicine:** 14,548 sessions conducted in FY2024. Sisaket, Kantharalak, and Phu Sing are top providers.
- **AI for Health:** AI-based Diabetic Retinopathy screening expanded to 100% of health districts.

## 4. Maternal, Child, and Elderly Health
- **Maternal Mortality:** 38.23 per 100,000 (Above national target). 
- **Infant Mortality:** 1.24 per 1,000 births (Well within target < 3.6).
- **Child IQ/EQ:** 95.26% of children have normal development.
- **Elderly (LTC):** 100% of community hospitals have Geriatric Clinics. 40.51% of palliative patients have Advance Care Plans.

## 5. Health Economy
- **Medical & Wellness Hub:** 51 wellness centers (hotels/restaurants/massage) certified.
- **Premium Massage:** 3 shops reached "Nuad Thai Premium" status.
