# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 2/2022 - Sisaket

## Overview
The second round inspection of fiscal year 2022 (7-9 June 2022) focused on post-pandemic recovery and the strengthening of public health governance.

## 1. Mental Health and Suicide Prevention
- **Sisaket Model:** Implementation of a provincial-wide monitoring system for suicide risk.
- **Statistics:** Suicide rate trended upward during the economic recovery phase. 2021 rate: 8.08 per 100k. 
- **Response:** "Mental Health Check-in" reached over 1.7 million participants. MCATT focus shifted to economic distress and its mental health consequences.

## 2. Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)
- **Hypertension (HT):** New cases incident rate: 1,136 per 100,000.
- **Diabetes (DM):** New cases incident rate: 4,345 per 100,000 (High due to intensive screening).
- **Community Models:** "New Normal Communities free from NCDs" launched in 22 pilot villages.

## 3. Elderly and Palliative Care
- **ADL Screening:** 95.86% of the elderly (220,090 people) screened for independence in daily living.
- **Dementia & Falls:** 54.29% of screened elderly showed early signs of cognitive decline; 58.25% at risk of falling.
- **Clinic:** 100% of community hospitals (22) have established Geriatric Clinics.

## 4. Maternal and Child Health
- **Maternal Mortality:** 42.90 per 100,000 births (Failed target). Action plan: "MCH Board" to monitor every case of bleeding and complication.
- **Child Nutrition:** 67.11% of children (0-5 years) are "High & Well-proportioned" (Passed target).
- **Development:** 78.33% of children with delayed development received intervention via TEDA4I.

## 5. Service Plan and Infrastructure
- **Intermediate Care (IMC):** Sisaket Hospital established an Intermediate Ward. Referral back system to community hospitals for rehabilitation.
- **Financial Risk:** 100% of hospitals are at Risk Level 0-2 (No financial crisis).
- **Digital Health:** Transition to HIS Gateway version 7. 100% of units are using digital reporting.
