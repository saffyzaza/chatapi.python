# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 1/2025 - Sisaket

## Overview
Inspection summary for fiscal year 2025 (February 2025). This report focuses on health service upgrades, NCD remission, and maternal/child health improvements.

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPI Summary)
- Total Indicators: 44
- **Passed:** 24 (55%)
- **Failed:** 13 (30%)
- **In Progress:** 7 (16%)

## 2. Advanced Health Policies
- **NCD Remission Clinic:** Sisaket is pioneering clinics to reverse Diabetes (DM) and Hypertension (HT). 100% of community hospitals have established these clinics.
- **Cyber Security (CTAM):** Evaluation of digital security. 2 hospitals reached high-level security; 7 at medium level. Target is 100% high-level by year-end.
- **Maternal and Child Health:**
    - Maternal Mortality: 38.62 per 100,000 (Target < 16). High risk due to indirect causes.
    - Child Development: 91.35% of children (0-5 years) have age-appropriate development.
    - IQ Target: Sisaket aims for an average IQ of 106+ by 2026.

## 3. Communicable Disease Management
- **OVCCA (Liver Fluke):** Screening using Quick Win (100 days). Prevalence of liver fluke infection in screened population is 3.04%.
- **Tuberculosis:** Treatment success rate for new cases in Q1 is 88.9%. Death rate among TB patients is 7.7%.
- **Stroke & Heart:**
    - Stroke Unit access within 72 hours: 80.36%.
    - STEMI Thrombolytic drug access: 97.44%.
    - STEMI Primary PCI access: 87.5%.

## 4. Special Groups and Vulnerable Populations
- **Prison Health:** 100% of inmates screened for TB, HIV, and Syphilis upon entry. Tele-dentistry and Tele-psychiatry are fully operational in prisons.
- **Elderly Care:**
    - 77.58% of palliative patients have Advance Care Plans.
    - Home Ward for active dying patients is available in 100% of target hospitals.
    - Caregiver production: 340 new caregivers trained in 2025.

## 5. Health Economy and Wellness
- **Medical & Wellness Hub:** Promotion of "Thai Traditional Premium" massage. Sisaket has 2 certified premium massage shops and 131 herbal/food products certified by Smart FDA.
- **Healthy Cities:** 22 districts are aiming for Healthy City status.

## 6. Financial Management (Good Governance)
- **Financial Risk:** No hospitals in Sisaket are at "Crisis Level" (Level 6-7). 4 hospitals have cash ratio issues (< 1.0) but are under monitoring.
- **Asset Management:** Focus on improving the GFMIS asset registration system.
