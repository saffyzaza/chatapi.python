# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 2/2023 - Sisaket

## Overview
Inspection results for the second round of fiscal year 2023 (21-23 June 2023). Focus on Digital Health, "Health for Wealth" initiative, and Cancer Anywhere.

## 1. Health for Wealth (Agenda Based)
- **Medical Cannabis:** 100% of community hospitals have Integrated Medical Cannabis Clinics. Patient access to cannabis for Palliative Care increased by 146.45%.
- **Wellness Economy:** Development of Wellness Centers in hotels and restaurants. Phran Sub-district (Khun Han) is a model for "Healthy Cities".
- **Herbal Products:** 40 local herbal products (Shallot, Garlic, Chili) received Smart FDA certification.

## 2. Digital Health
- **Tele-medicine:** 22/22 hospitals implemented Tele-medicine. 19 use the "GLINK" system developed by Sisaket Hospital.
- **Digital ID:** 83.20% of health personnel and 4.14% of the general public registered for Digital Health IDs.
- **AI Integration:** Deployment of AI for Diabetic Retinopathy (AI DR) screening in target districts.

## 3. Specialized Service Plans (Functional Based)
- **Stroke:** 92.69% of stroke patients receive treatment in Stroke Units within 72 hours.
- **STEMI (Heart):** 77.32% of STEMI patients received thrombolytics within the standard time frame.
- **Cancer Management:**
    - Cervical Cancer: 20.19% screening coverage using HPV DNA test (FY2023).
    - Colorectal Cancer: 26.96% screening coverage via Fit Test. 98.57% of positive cases received Colonoscopy.
- **Mental Health:** Suicide rate: 4.87 per 100,000 population. 98% of attempters do not repeat within 1 year.

## 4. Regional Health Issues (Area Based)
- **OVCCA:** 1.97% prevalence of liver fluke in tested population. 16/22 districts have built sewage management systems.
- **Child IQ:** Target is IQ > 103 by 2026. 95.26% of children screened had age-appropriate development.
