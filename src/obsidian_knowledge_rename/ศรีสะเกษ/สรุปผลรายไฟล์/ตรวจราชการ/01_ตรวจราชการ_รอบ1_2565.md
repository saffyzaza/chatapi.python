# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 1/2022 - Sisaket

## Overview
This document summarizes the results of the first round of public health inspection and supervision in Sisaket Province for the fiscal year 2022 (9-11 March 2022). It covers the three main areas: Agenda-based, Functional-based, and Area-based issues.

## 1. Royal Projects and Honor Projects
- **Rajathan Punsook Project:** Focuses on improving healthcare access for inmates in Sisaket Provincial Prison and Kantharalak District Prison. 
    - **Inmate Stats:** Sisaket Prison (1,861 inmates), Kantharalak Prison (1,194 inmates).
    - **Disease Profile:** HIV/AIDS, Tuberculosis, Syphilis, Psychiatric issues, Hypertension, and Diabetes are common.
    - **Innovation:** Implementation of **Tele-dentistry** to provide oral health care to inmates during COVID-19.
- **Monk Health Project:** Screening for monks and novices across 22 districts. 1,732 monks screened.
- **Child Nutrition in Remote Areas (KPD):** Monitoring nutritional status in border and remote areas.

## 2. Public Health Resources and Demographics
- **Population:** 1,457,556 (2021 data).
- **Health Infrastructure:** 1 Tertiary Hospital (A), 1 General Hospital (M1), 5 Community Hospitals (M2/F1), 14 District Hospitals (F2), and 253 Sub-district Health Promoting Hospitals (SHPH).
- **Workforce:** 437 Doctors, 130 Dentists, 199 Pharmacists, 2,444 Nurses, 1,003 Public Health Officers.

## 3. Key Health Situations (2021)
- **Top Causes of Outpatient (OPD) Visits:** 1. Hypertension, 2. Acute Respiratory Infection, 3. Diabetes.
- **Top Causes of Inpatient (IPD) Admissions:** 1. Pneumonia, 2. Acute Tonsillitis, 3. Diabetes.
- **Top Causes of Death:** 1. Cancer (Liver and Bile Duct), 2. Pneumonia, 3. Sepsis, 4. Stroke.
- **Suicide:** Sisaket shows an increasing trend. 2021 rate: 8.08 per 100,000 population. 13 districts are marked as high-risk areas.

## 4. Performance on Key Indicators
- **Primary Care Development:** 60 units registered (41.95%). Targets for 2022 include adding 12 more teams.
- **District Quality of Life (DQC):** All 22 districts passed the UCCARE criteria.
- **Elderly Care (LTC):** Screening for Geriatric Syndromes. 100% of districts have Long-Term Care systems in place.
- **Cancer Management:** Liver and Bile Duct cancer is the leading concern. Screening using Ultrasound reached 73.69% of the target group in 2021.
- **COVID-19:** Massive vaccination campaign and establishment of field hospitals in prisons.

## 5. Service Plan and Specialized Care
- **Heart (STEMI):** Cath Lab available since March 2021.
- **Trauma/ER:** Implementation of ER Service Delivery targets (2-4 hours wait time).
- **Intermediate Care (IMC):** Development of 3D Indoor Walking innovation for physical therapy in collaboration with Sisaket Rajabhat University.
