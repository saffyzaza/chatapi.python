# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 2/2021 - Sisaket

## Overview
This report focuses on the secondary inspection round of fiscal year 2021 (7-9 July 2021) in Sisaket. The primary concern during this period was the management of the COVID-19 pandemic and its impact on regular health services.

## 1. COVID-19 Situation and Response
- **Inmate Outbreaks:** Sisaket Provincial Prison and Kantharalak District Prison faced significant outbreaks. Field hospitals were established within prison grounds to manage infected inmates.
- **Mental Health Impact:** The pandemic led to increased stress and depression among the general population and health workers. The MCATT team was deployed to monitor high-risk groups (those in quarantine, post-discharge patients).
- **Vaccination:** Launch of the "Mor Phrom" and "Sisaket Phrom" apps for vaccine registration.

## 2. Royal and Special Projects
- **TO BE NUMBER ONE:** Sisaket has been highly successful, with 96.69% of youth (6+) being members. The province aims to be a "Diamond Level" model by 2026.
- **Rajathan Punsook:** Continued development of healthcare systems for inmates, focusing on infectious disease control (TB, HIV) and dental care.
- **PO.SO.WA. (Mobile Medical Units):** Provided services to 11 remote villages, serving 4,384 people. Common issues: Dental, Respiratory, and Musculoskeletal disorders.

## 3. Disease Control (OVCCA)
- **Liver Fluke & Bile Duct Cancer:** average prevalence of liver fluke over 5 years is 4.21%. In 2021, screening detected liver fluke in 2.23% of the tested population.
- **Sanitation:** 3 districts (Wang Hin, Khukhan, Sri Rattana) have implemented waste/sewage management systems to break the liver fluke cycle.

## 4. Health Indicators (2020-2021)
- **Birth Rate:** 6.67 per 1,000 (Decreasing trend).
- **Death Rate:** 6.64 per 1,000 (Increasing trend).
- **Infant Mortality:** 4.37 per 1,000 births.
- **Maternal Mortality:** 35.68 per 100,000 births (Increased from previous years).
- **Top Death Causes:** 1. Cancer, 2. Pneumonia, 3. Sepsis.

## 5. Innovation and Primary Care
- **Tele-medicine:** Initial deployment of telemedicine for chronic patients and inmates.
- **Primary Care (PCU/NPCU):** 58 teams registered. Targeted 20 more for the end of 2021.
- **Elderly Care:** ADL screening reached 95.86% of the target elderly population (200,869 people).
