# ULTRA DETAILED: Provincial Inspection Report Round 1/2024 - Sisaket

## Overview
Results of the first round of provincial inspection for Sisaket (21-23 February 2024). This report highlights the transition to the **"30 Baht Everywhere"** policy and advancements in digital health.

## 1. Strategic Health Policies (Agenda Based)
- **30 Baht Everywhere Policy:** Sisaket is preparing for full implementation, enabling citizens to receive treatment anywhere using a single ID card.
- **Smart Hospitals:** Promotion of the "One ID Card Smart Hospital" system. Current success: 20/22 hospitals are online with HIS Gateway.
- **Mental Health & Drugs:** 
    - Establishment of **MINI Thanyarak** centers in Phosri Suwan, Sri Rattana, and Prang Ku for integrated psychiatric and drug treatment.
    - Suicide Prevention: "HOPE Task Force" and "Mental Health Check-in" programs. Sisaket suicide rate remains a concern at 7.41 per 100,000 (2023).

## 2. Disease Control and Prevention
- **Cancer (มะเร็งครบวงจร):** 
    - Cervical Cancer: HPV DNA testing screening target is 60%. Sisaket received a "Gold Award" for HPV vaccination coverage in youth (11-20 years).
    - Liver & Bile Duct Cancer (OVCCA): Screening 40+ years old population using Ultrasound (Result: 25.42% of target).
    - Colorectal Cancer: Fit test screening (Result: 12.71% coverage).
- **Tuberculosis (TB):** Coverage rate for 2024 is 28.1% (Incidence 43.5 per 100,000). Success rate for new smear-positive cases is 88.33%.
- **Communicable Diseases (2023):** Top infections include COVID-19 (908.89 per 100,000), Pneumonia, and Diarrhea.

## 3. Primary Care and Health Resources
- **PCU/NPCU Establishment:** 88/140 teams (62.86%) registered. 11 districts have achieved full coverage.
- **Health Station & Smart VHV:** 27,209 VHVs in Sisaket. 99.19% are using the "Smart VHV" app for reporting.
- **NCD Management:** Screening for Hypertension (34.10%) and Diabetes (35.15%). Control rates for existing patients: DM (30.38%), HT (49.47%).

## 4. Specialized Care (Functional Based)
- **Stroke:** Sisaket Hospital (Level A) has a 12-bed Stroke Unit. 80.36% of patients reach the unit within 72 hours.
- **Heart (STEMI):** 83.33% of STEMI patients receive treatment within the standard time.
- **Intermediate Care (IMC):** Expansion of home-based rehabilitation.

## 5. Health Economy (Wellness Hub)
- **Healthy Cities Models:** Phran Sub-district (Khun Han) is the provincial model.
- **Wellness Centers:** Target of 16 centers; 6 hospitals and 6 restaurants have already passed criteria.
- **Thai Traditional Medicine:** Promotion of herbal products (Smart Products).
